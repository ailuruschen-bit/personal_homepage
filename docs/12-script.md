# 12 Script

**Status: proposed, and the lines are drafts.** The shooting script: what the
camera does, what is on screen and what is said, scene by scene.
[10](10-scroll-narrative.md) defines the camera;
[11](11-premise-and-voice.md) defines the premise and the rules the writing
obeys.

When the lines are final they move into `content/`, and this page keeps only
the structure.

## Conventions

The prose is English, like every other page here. **The lines are Chinese**,
because they are content rather than documentation.

```
[A]  anchored   a function of scroll offset; replays on a second pass
[R]  reactive   answers the pointer or idling; plays once per trigger
[L]  live       after the badge reads LIVE
[  ] placeholder — needs a real fact and is not mine to invent
```

## Cast

| Who    | What they are                                                         |
| ------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 侦探   | hired, experienced, unimpressed. Heard, never seen. Wears the camera. |
| 他     | the occupant. A silhouette in a doorway, then a voice.                |
| 委托人 | the reader. Never named, never addressed until the door.              |

## The setting

A night train between two cities, after midnight. A private sleeper
compartment with the light left on, a cup still steaming, a laptop still open.
Its occupant is in the vestibule taking a phone call, because that is where
calls are taken on a train.

The detective comes down the corridor from the next carriage. He is wearing a
camera; the client asked to see for themselves. What the reader watches is that
feed, with a buffer, which is why it rewinds.

The timecode is a function of scroll. The whole visit runs under ten minutes
however long it is read.

## Scene 0 — In

**Segment:** the opening, locked. **Camera:** as built — the blinds tilt, the
camera goes out through the window to the city. **Chrome:** 回放,
`00:00:00`.

```
[A] 这种锁，十年前就该换了。
[A] 人在连廊接电话。守规矩的乘客，对我这行最友好。
[A] 咖啡还冒着气。他以为自己一分钟就回来。
```

The first line is the character: he has seen this lock before and is offended
on the railway's behalf. The third plants "一分钟", which the timecode then
contradicts in silence for the rest of the site.

## Scene 1 — The window

**Segment:** cover. **Camera:** outside, parallax only.

```
[A] 景色不错。
[A] 可惜委托人不是花钱请我看风景的。
```

The second line is the scroll affordance, and the only time the client is
mentioned before the door.

## Scene 2 — The compartment

**Segment:** porch, window transit inward, hub. Pointer focus is live.

```
[A] 一个人的铺位。东西摆得像随时准备跑路。
[A] 这种人我见得多了。要么赶时间，要么心里有鬼。

[R] 车窗        看风景的时间，委托人不报销。
[R] 墙上线路图  铁路公司的线路图。公家的东西，问不出什么。
[R] 咖啡        还烫。别碰——杯子挪了位置，比指纹还难解释。
[R] 电脑        屏幕还亮着。现在的人连锁屏都懒得按。
[R] idle        我站着也不会有人替我翻。
[R] 看过的      又是它。我记性还没差到那个地步。
```

The wall map and the coffee are decoys: they take focus, earn a line, and open
nothing. The idle line is the site's only nudge, and it nudges in character.

## Scene 3 — 车票 (about)

**Camera:** carry, up off the table. **Scroll:** turns it over halfway through.

**Artifact, front:** `[出发地] → [目的地]` · `[日期]` · `[座位]`.
**Artifact, back:** the card behind it — `[姓名]` · `[职位]` · `[联系方式]`.

```
[A] open   一张票，折过两次，边上起毛了。
[A] flip   后面夹着名片。省了我半天功夫。
[A] close  位置差一厘米他都看得出来。这种人我惹不起。
```

Where he is going is on the front; who he is is on the back. The flip line is
a professional's relief at being handed the answer.

## Scene 4 — 卷起来的线路图 (journey)

**Camera:** carry, out of the luggage rack. **Scroll:** unrolls it. Stations
appear in order as it opens; each is `[经历]`.

```
[A] open    自己带的线路图。橡皮筋都勒出印子了。这年头还有人用纸。
[A] unroll  手绘的。……行吧，是个讲究人。
[A] close   一个随身带着自己走过的路的人。要么念旧，要么还没走到地方。
```

The reader is scrolling a scroll. Nothing needs to point that out. The close
offers two readings and picks neither.

## Scene 5 — 公文包 (stack)

**Camera:** carry, down off the rack. **Scroll:** draws the books out one at a
time. Spines are `[技术栈]`; wear is proficiency; the last has a bookmark in
it and is `[正在学习]`.

```
[A] open   没上锁。打开之前我总盼着里面有点刺激的。
[A] books  书。全是书。磨得最狠的那几本，书名我都念不顺。
[A] last   最后一本夹着书签。人总得有点没看完的东西。
[A] close  摆得整整齐齐。这人要是干我这行，早就发财了。
```

The open is a setup that the whole chapter pays off: he wanted something
exciting and got a shelf.

## Scene 6 — 电脑 (projects)

**Camera:** dolly. **Scroll:** the progress bar of the job he left running is
the chapter's reading progress. The tabs are `[项目]`.

```
[A] open   屏幕没锁，连睡眠都没进。他是真的以为自己一分钟就回来。
[A] tabs   开着的全是公开仓库。家底都挂在网上了，我还潜进来干什么。
[A] close  我看不懂他在写什么。但看得懂一个人半夜还在写。
```

The middle line is the detective discovering he was unnecessary, which is the
truest thing in the script: it is a public site.

**The close is the one sincere line, and it stays the only one.** It says what
the occupant could not say about himself.

## Scene 7 — 笔记 and 兴趣

**Blocked on artwork.** They sit between scenes 6 and 8. A veteran who has just
been moved by a laptop needs to be wrong about something immediately
afterwards, and the hobby object is where.

## Scene 8 — The door

**Camera:** the site's only cut-in — it turns to the door. **Chrome:** the
timecode reads `00:09:47`.

```
[A] 门响了。
[A] 干这行学会的第一件事：门响的时候，别回头。
[A] ……我还是回头了。
```

The third beat turns the camera. The badge changes from 回放 to LIVE.

```
[L] 他    ……你坐错车厢了吧？
[L] 他    还录着呢。那正好——不管是谁在看，想知道什么，直接问我。
[L] 侦探  ……那我的尾款还结吗？
```

The first is an understatement from a man who keeps his ticket to the
millimetre. The second is him taking the camera, and it is the chat section's
opening prompt: the reader is now being spoken to, about the thing they came
for. The third is optional and the first to cut.

## Scene 9 — The last frame

**Camera:** the window transit outward, as built. He has put the camera down
facing the window.

```
[A] 侦探  下一趟车我买票。
[A] 他    景色不错吧。
```

The site ends on its own first line, in the other man's mouth. The footer is
out here.

### Devices in use

- **The turn.** Flat observation, short undercut. Lines 3, 10, 16, 23.
- **The silent joke.** "一分钟" in lines 3 and 24, against a timecode that
  reads nine by the door. Nobody remarks on it.
- **The delayed payoff.** Line 20 sets up, the chapter pays off.
- **The decoy.** Lines 9 and 10: things that are only things.
- **The gesture is the object.** Scene 4: scrolling a scroll.
- **The deflation.** Line 25: the detective learns the job was unnecessary.
- **One sincere line.** Line 26.
- **The stolen line.** Line 34 is line 4, in the other man's mouth.
